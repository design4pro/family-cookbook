## Question

Who owns and pays for the cookbook, how many people share it, and does the first pilot include web, iOS and Android? Two live questions were sent to the user. Do not treat recommended answers as acceptance.

## Context

[MVP specification](../spec.md). Earlier evidence: [AI research](../../cookbook-ai-features-cloudflare-research.md) and [opportunity research](../../family-cookbook-opportunity-research.md).

## Comments

Opening round, 2026-10-15: asked whether to target a shared five-person household with one payer or a personal book; asked whether to cover web/iOS/Android in a closed pilot or launch web first. Recommended the shared household and closed cross-platform pilot. No answer has been received in this session; the ticket remains open and unclaimed.


## Answer

Resolution comment, 2026-10-15: the user explicitly chose a shared family cookbook for up to five people total, with separate accounts and one payer. The user also chose web, iOS and Android, validated first through a closed pilot before public release.

This acceptance does not approve the proposed EUR 39 price, quotas, retention, Polish-first localization, detailed permissions, the one-household-per-user constraint or the rest of the technical proposal. Those remain with the relevant open decision tickets. Ownership transfer and payer replacement remain part of the payment/continuity decision.

Context: [accepted boundaries in the proposal](../spec.md#1-product-and-scope); [domain vocabulary](../CONTEXT.md).
