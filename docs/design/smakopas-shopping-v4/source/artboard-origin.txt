Authored static shopping-list design study for Family Cookbook, a working name. Requested scope: web desktop/mobile, iPhone, iPad and Android phone; preserve the established Smakopas v4 typography, proportions and simplicity, with a static Liquid Glass projection on Apple controls and Material-style Android controls. No image-generation model was used. SVG geometry and text were composed by source/render.cjs with source/web.cjs, source/apple.cjs and source/android.cjs, then rasterized with Sharp/librsvg at 2x. Archivo content, local SF Pro Text on Apple chrome, bundled Roboto on Android chrome. SF Symbols were exported locally through AppKit; Material Symbols came from the preceding Android v4 packet. Quantities and state relationships are synthetic demo data specified in source/scenario.json. This is review artwork, not implemented UI, native runtime evidence, user approval or proof of persistence, accessibility or synchronization.
