## Visual direction

Archivo supplies the distinctive reading titles and recipe content. Bundled Roboto Variable supplies Android controls, labels and supporting text. Both fonts are real assets; the SVGs embed them and the renderer loads them from the local asset directory. [Credits](credits.md) records their provenance and licenses.

White, ink `#1c1e21`, muted text `#5b636b`, rules `#dde3e6`, surface `#eef1f2` and accent `#0e7c70` retain the Smakopas v4 direction. The Android study adds an outlined-field color `#747e83`, tonal action container `#d8eee8` and dark container text `#104b43`. Amber `#855119` accompanies explicit missing-amount text. Reading pages and form surfaces remain opaque.

Actual Material Symbols Outlined SVG assets supply the icons. Navigation uses opaque Material surfaces, a selected indicator and visible labels. There is no SF Pro, SF Symbols or Apple Liquid Glass treatment. The [Material reference](material-reference.md) separates verified platform guidance from this study's product decisions.

## Navigation and screen composition

Cookbook, Assistant and Shopping are the three bottom destinations on browsing and saved-recipe screens. Add recipe is a 56-unit-high extended FAB above the navigation bar, not a destination. The focused Add photos and Review screens cover destination navigation and provide a leading Back control. Review also exposes a trailing Save action.

Top app bars occupy 64 logical units below the illustrative status area. Review has 48-unit-high Source / Draft tabs. Outlined review fields are 56 units high. Intended interactive regions are at least 48 x 48 logical units; [target-regions.json](source/target-regions.json) records the drawing's nominal regions, not native hit testing or accessible semantics. The 24-unit status and gesture areas describe one static device configuration, not fixed implementation insets.

## Content continuity and intended behavior

The initial cookbook contains existing public demonstration recipes. Family pancakes is absent before import. The Shakshuka and Greek salad photographs remain demo-recipe imagery; they do not become a cover or original for the imported recipe.

Capture uses the same two 600 x 760 synthetic originals as [web v4](../smakopas-web-v4/capture-brief.md). Their PNG files are byte-identical copies. The drawing embeds each complete image proportionally without cropping or changing its contents.

- Both source pages are titled `Family pancakes` and say `Recipe by Anna`.
- Page 1 contains `2½ cups flour`, `2 eggs`, `250 ml milk` and `salt`.
- Page 2 contains `Mix the ingredients.` and `Cook in a pan.`
- Both sources are visibly synthetic. Anna is the fictional recipe author; Alex is the fictional source uploader and application editor. Their roles remain separately labeled.
- Salt quantity and servings remain `Not specified`. Portion scaling remains unavailable. The optional family note is empty. No time, temperature, serving count, family story or dish photograph is invented.

Add photos shows two selected files that have not been uploaded. `Uses 2 image pages` describes this operation without inventing an account balance. The instruction before upload asks the user to keep the screen open until upload finishes; processing can continue after leaving once upload completes.

Review marks the content as an AI draft that is not yet in the cookbook. It asks the user to check every field, including fields without a warning. The form provides title, attribution, servings, optional note, ingredient quantities/names and numbered steps, with add/remove affordances. Source access preserves the unchanged originals. A missing quantity may remain unknown after review.

Save represents explicit human approval and makes the recipe available to the household. Transcription, switching tabs and the absence of a warning do not establish approval. Cooking and recipe questions use approved content. Add ingredients represents entry to review and confirmation of shopping additions, not an immediate update to the shared list. None of these painted controls performs the depicted operation.

## Review and validation limits

The packet depicts a phone in light appearance at normal font size. Android tablets, foldables and Dark Mode are outside this packet. The exports do not establish native control fidelity, accessibility compliance or runtime behavior. The independent confirmation resolved the one reported clipping defect; it does not establish user approval.

When implementation is explicitly authorized, validate real window/cutout/keyboard insets, system and predictive Back, gesture and three-button navigation, text and display scaling, Polish labels, field wrapping, focus, TalkBack labels/order and preservation of edits when switching tabs. Check actual hit targets and content clearance beneath fixed controls. Future adaptive and Dark Mode studies must establish their own layouts and evidence.

Retain the existing requirements for upload recovery, processing failures, permissions, quotas, revision conflicts and source preservation. Static drawings do not test these states. T03 physical-device durability and T09 capture-fidelity/evaluation gaps remain open; neither readiness gate changes.
